Importance is the tax on the wish

Excess potential is not a metaphor. The more you inflate the goal, the more the field shoves back.

2 min

Zeland calls it excess potential. You can call it making a god out of a Tuesday.

You want the thing. Fine. Then you add: if I do not get it I am nobody, my mother was right, the demolition was for nothing, I cannot enjoy a meal. That extra charge is the tax. Reality does not punish you. Your nervous system starts steering by panic, and panic is a bad driver.

Two kinds of wanting

Clean want: I would like this. I will walk toward it. If it misses I still eat.

Dirty want: I need this to prove the story. Every delay is a verdict. You check the phone like a trader. The neck files the report the mouth will not say.

Dirty want feels like devotion. It is just inflation.

How it shows in a Taurus body

Fixed earth holds. That is a gift for building. It is a trap for importance. You sit on the goal until it becomes furniture, then you call the furniture destiny.

A Life Path 5 next to that is a joke the sky already told: freedom versus the vault. When 5 is starved, 8-style control shows up anyway. You manage other people’s empires because you will not claim your own without a courtroom.

Practical drop

You do not drop the aim. You drop 30 percent of the story.

  • Say the aim once, out loud, without a speech.
  • Do one physical step.
  • Put the phone in another room for an hour.
  • If anger arrives, it is usually a boundary you did not speak. Speak it to a wall first if you must. Then to the person. Silent anger is a pendulum with your face on it.

Hawkins people will hear “let go.” Same move, different dialect. You feel the charge in the body until it is just sensation, not a lawsuit.

One truth

The goal is rarely the problem. The altar you built under it is. Take the flowers off. Keep walking.
