Physics of manifestation

A clean aim, a dropped tax, and feet. The genie is literal. Wishing is inner intention with perfume.

1 min

What is the physics of manifestation?

Not “think and it appears.” A sequence:

  1. Name the aim in one sentence. Precise. “A life partner” is not the same order as “a life I can enjoy in my body on a Tuesday.”
  2. Drop 30 percent of the importance. Excess potential is the tax.
  3. Take the next physical step a non-mystic would take.
  4. When the neck spikes, that is the tax arriving. Soften. Do not abandon the aim.

Inner intention pushes the world. Outer intention walks where the track already is.

What this is not

Not a guarantee. Not a replacement for work. Not a reason to skip the doctor or the invoice. Manifestation that cannot pay a bill is a mood.

One truth

Keep the aim. Put the altar down. Walk. The Tuesday is a result.
