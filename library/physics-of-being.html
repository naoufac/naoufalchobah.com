Physics of being

Being is the charge you sit in when nobody is watching. Not a brand. A state.

1 min

What is the physics of being?

Being is not a personality. It is the charge in the chair when the audience left. You can fake a sun-sign costume. You cannot fake sleep.

Hawkins: a level is what the system can do. Shame hides. Pride argues. Courage moves. Love includes.

Zeland: a slide is the picture you sit inside. If the slide is “I am not allowed to enjoy,” the body will audit every meal.

How to read your own being

  1. Notice the first sensation, before the story.
  2. Name the story once. Then drop the lawsuit.
  3. See if the next action is smaller or cleaner.

If a practice makes you more obsessed and smaller, it is a pendulum. If it makes the clerk quieter and the step cleaner, it is being.

One truth

You are not the press release. You are the night shift. Sit with that, and the day gets honest.
