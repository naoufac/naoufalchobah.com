Transurfing

Pendulums are not your friends

Transurfing in one cut. A pendulum feeds on your importance. Drop the fight and it loses the hook.

1 min

A pendulum is a thought-form that lives on your attention. News. A couple fight. A career identity. It does not care if you love it or hate it. Charge is food.

Vadim Zeland’s move is not to smash it. Smashing is more charge. The move is to see the hook, reduce importance, and step sideways.

Outer intention is not wishing. It is alignment plus action without the drama of “this must happen or I die.”

If a practice makes you smaller and more obsessed, it is a pendulum wearing spiritual clothes.
